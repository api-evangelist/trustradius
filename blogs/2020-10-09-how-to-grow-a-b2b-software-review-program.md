With the launch of the first annual 2020 People of Color in Tech Report, we wanted to continue the conversation around diversity and inclusion. As a company dedicated to transparency and integrity, we wanted to highlight the experiences of marginalized groups in the tech industry. Join our panel of executives for an open conversation about: How to break into tech and overcome barriers to success New Diversity & Inclusion research for the tech industry Practical takeaways for how to address diversity Global conversations around racial inequality have spiked in interest this year and companies can no longer ignore how the inequalities in their very own industries contribute to the marginalization of certain populations outside of their businesses. We hope this conversation and our report can aid tech leaders, enabling their organizations to become centers of innovation driven by true inclusivity.
